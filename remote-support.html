Fix it without leaving home

A lot of common computer problems don't require a trip to the shop. Printer not working, email acting up, software crashes, slow performance, setting changes — these are all things we can handle remotely while you watch, in real time.

We use Splashtop SOS, a secure and professional remote access tool. You stay in control the entire time — you can see everything we're doing on your screen, and the session ends the moment we're done.

  • Printer installation and troubleshooting
  • Email setup and configuration
  • Software installation and updates
  • Slow computer tune-up
  • Virus and malware removal
  • Windows errors and crashes
  • Browser issues and cleanup
  • Network and Wi-Fi troubleshooting
  • General how-to guidance and training

Secure and straightforward

Splashtop SOS creates an encrypted, one-time session. We never have permanent access to your computer — every session requires your permission to start, and ends when you close it.
