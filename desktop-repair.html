Upgrades worth considering

Sometimes a relatively small investment can dramatically extend the life of your desktop. Here are the upgrades we recommend most often.

SSD Upgrade

Swapping an old spinning hard drive for a solid-state drive is the single biggest speed improvement you can make. Boot times drop from minutes to seconds.

RAM Upgrade

If your desktop slows to a crawl with multiple tabs or programs open, more RAM is often the answer. Fast, affordable, and immediately noticeable.

Full Cleaning

Dust buildup is a silent killer for desktops — it causes overheating, crashes, and premature hardware failure. A thorough cleaning can add years to a machine's life.
