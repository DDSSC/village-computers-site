We recover what matters most

Your computer holds more than data — it holds photos, documents, years of work, and memories that can't be replaced. When a drive fails or a laptop stops booting, the situation feels urgent and scary. We understand that, and we treat every recovery job with the care it deserves.

We handle a wide range of data loss scenarios, from simple file recoveries to more complex drive failures. Once we've assessed the situation, we'll give you a clear estimate before any work begins — no surprises.

  • Hard drive failure (mechanical and electronic)
  • SSD and flash storage recovery
  • Laptop won't power on or boot
  • Accidental file or folder deletion
  • Corrupted or inaccessible drives
  • Water or physical damage assessment
  • Data transfer to a new device
  • Backup setup to prevent future loss

Free basic diagnostics — no-fix, no-charge

We never charge you for a basic diagnosis. If we can't recover your data, you don't pay. We'll always tell you upfront what's recoverable and what isn't, so you can make the best decision for your situation.

Most standard recoveries are completed within 24–48 hours. For drives with physical damage, timelines may vary — we'll keep you updated every step of the way.

Don't make it worse

If your drive is making clicking or grinding noises, power it off immediately and don't try to run recovery software on it. Bring it in — physical damage requires hands-on assessment.
