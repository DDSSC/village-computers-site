Signs your computer may be infected

Not sure if you have a virus? Here are the most common warning signs.

Running unusually slow

Malware often runs in the background consuming CPU and memory, making everything sluggish.

Pop-ups and ads everywhere

Unexpected pop-ups, especially ones warning about viruses or asking you to call a number, are a red flag.

Browser acting strangely

New toolbars, a changed homepage, or searches redirecting to unfamiliar sites are signs of a browser hijacker.

Programs crashing or freezing

Frequent crashes, blue screens, or programs that won't open can indicate system-level infection.

Files missing or encrypted

If files have strange extensions or you're seeing ransom messages, you may have ransomware — stop using the computer and call us.

Antivirus turned off

Some malware disables your antivirus software to avoid detection. If yours has stopped working, that's a serious sign.
