Real repairs, not just replacements

A lot of shops will tell you a laptop isn't worth fixing and push you toward buying a new one. We take a different approach — we diagnose the actual problem, tell you exactly what it'll cost to fix, and let you decide. No pressure, no upsell.

We're comfortable doing hardware-level repairs that many shops shy away from, including LCD replacements, power jack repairs, and motherboard-level diagnostics. If it can be fixed, we'll fix it.

  • Broken or cracked LCD / screen replacement
  • Power jack repair (laptop won't charge)
  • Battery replacement
  • Keyboard replacement
  • Overheating — fan cleaning and thermal paste replacement
  • Slow laptop tune-up and optimization
  • Operating system reinstall / Windows repair
  • RAM and SSD upgrades
  • Hinge repair
  • Water damage assessment

Free basic diagnostics — no-fix, no-charge

We don't charge for basic diagnostics. If we can't fix your laptop or the repair isn't worth the cost, you won't pay a dime. We'll always give you an honest assessment so you can make the right call.
