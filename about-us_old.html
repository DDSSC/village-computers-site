About Village Computers

Honest, affordable IT support for the Greater Charleston area — because your computer is more than just a computer.

More than silicon and plastic

Your computer holds pieces of your life — photos, work, years of memories, and things that can't be replaced. When something goes wrong, we understand that we're not just fixing equipment. We're getting that back to you. That understanding shapes everything we do.

Village Computers LLC is the evolution of CyndrTec, a name that long-time Charleston-area clients may recognize. Same team, same values, same commitment to treating every client with patience and respect — just a name that better reflects who we are and what we do.

We're located in Mount Pleasant on Warrick Lane, right off Ben Sawyer Blvd, across from Harris Teeter and Goodwill. We serve clients across the Greater Charleston area, both in-shop and on-site, and remotely across the country.

We believe good IT support should be accessible to everyone — not just businesses with big budgets. That means fair pricing, honest assessments, and never charging you for something you don't need. If a repair isn't worth the cost, we'll tell you. If we can't fix it, you don't pay.

Plain English, always

We've answered more than 15,000 questions from clients over the years, and we've learned that people don't want to be talked down to — they want to understand what's going on with their technology. We explain everything clearly, welcome questions, and won't leave until you're confident and comfortable.

What we stand for

These aren't just talking points — they're how we actually operate, every day.

Honesty first

We tell you what's actually wrong, what it'll cost, and whether the repair makes sense. No inflated diagnoses, no unnecessary upsell.

Fair pricing

Free basic diagnostics on every job. If we can't fix it, you don't pay. We price our work to be accessible, not to maximize every ticket.

Fast turnaround

We know your computer is important to your day. Most repairs are completed within 24–48 hours, and we keep you updated along the way.

Plain English

We explain things in terms that make sense. You'll always know what we did and why — and we genuinely welcome your questions.

Customer first

Client satisfaction is the primary mission on every job, every visit. We're not done until you're happy.

Community rooted

We're a local business in Mount Pleasant. Our clients are our neighbors, and we treat them that way.
